08Questions

Asked, answered.

How long does a project take?

Each service above shows a typical turnaround. Small, well-defined jobs usually take days; larger builds take a few weeks. Your written quote includes a delivery date before anything starts.

How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds is written into every quote, so you know it up front. If a change grows the scope, we quote it first. Nothing is added silently.

How do I pay?

Usually through a freelance platform such as Fiverr or Upwork, so the platform holds the payment and its protection covers both sides. If you would rather work directly, the payment terms are agreed in the written quote.

Who owns the finished work?

You do, once the project is paid. Source files, code, setup notes and any accounts we created are handed over. Nothing stays locked to us.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

Usually, yes. We build on what you already have where it makes sense, and tell you plainly when something else would serve you better.

What do you need from me to start?

A few lines about the outcome you want, any examples you like, and your deadline. We take it from there with a short scope chat.
